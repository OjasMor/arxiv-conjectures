DETAILS: No proof step fails.

1. **The pure power of \(z\).** After the first derivative, each column is generated by \(u_j\phi(u_jz)\), an exponential in \(t=z^2/2\). In row \(r\), the coefficient of \(\partial_t^r\) in \(\partial_z^r\) is \(z^r\); all other terms have lower derivative order. Thus the row transformation contributes exactly \(z^{\binom n2}\), with no lower powers in the determinant. This argument does not divide by \(z\), so it also covers \(z=0\). The Vandermonde nodes are \(-u_j^2\), giving the stated orientation \(u_p^2-u_q^2\).
2. **Confluence and coefficient.** The difference quotient is taken on whole columns. Since the derivatives commute and \(\partial_s\Phi(sz)=z\phi(sz)\), it gives precisely the missing column in each pair. Each within-pair difference contributes \(-2s_i\); the two column prefactors together contribute \(s_i^2\phi(s_i z)^2\). Between blocks the exponents are \(4\) or \(2\), and the singleton contributes \(s_K\phi(s_Kz)\). This accounts for every factor, including the Gaussian factors removed in the reduction.
3. **Sign.** Moving the second column of pair \(i\) past the \(K-i\) later \(\Phi\)-columns costs \(\binom K2\) transpositions in total. Together with the \(K-1\) negative within-pair factors, this gives \((-1)^{\binom K2+K-1}=(-1)^{\lfloor(K-1)/2\rfloor}\), exactly the appendix sign.
